Title

Victrola and "Yellow Dog Blues" advertisement

Description

Here, the song "Yellow Dog Blues" is used to help advertise a Victrola record player. This advertisement demonstrates how popular W.C. Handy's song was at the time and how it was strongly associated as dancing music.

Date

November 30, 1919

Library of Congress Chronicling America
