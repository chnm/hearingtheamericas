      An advertisement published in 1917 in the New Orleans Item announcing the first jazz record by the Original Dixieland Jazz Band on the Victor record label. The ad includes the word &quot;jass&quot; rather than jazz, demonstrating the early spelling before the genre formalized. The ad describes jass music as &quot;A Brass Band Gone Crazy!&quot; and admits that &quot;Beyond that description we can&#039;t tell you what a &#039;Jass&#039; Band is because we don&#039;t know for ourselves.&quot;<br />
      <br />
