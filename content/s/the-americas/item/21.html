Cal Stewart

About

Cal Stewart was a famous vaudeville star in the 19th and 20th century. He is best remembered for playing the character of "Uncle Josh Weathersby from Way Down East," a caricature of a rural rube character. In the 1890s, Edison hired Stewart to record a number of cylinders of his humorous Uncle Josh character to great popularity.
