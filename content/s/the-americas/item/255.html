Title

Alberta Hunter advertisement

Description

This advertisement for Puritan Records showcases Alberta Hunter and her new song "Don't Talk About Me." The lyrics are included in the ad, where Hunter pleads with her ex, "If you don't like my loving, just keep it to yourself."

Date

February 24, 1923

Source
