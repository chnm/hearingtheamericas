Title

Advertisement for Columbia Records mentioning "The Argentines, the Portuguese and the Greeks"

Description

This advertisement for Columbia Records mentions "The Argentines, the Portuguese and the Greeks," a song epitomizing ethnic humor and celebration. Yet the ad's image directly evokes the minstrel stage, depicting a man in blackface dressed in a tuxedo behind a male performer on a stage.

Date

October 28, 1920
