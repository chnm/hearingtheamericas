Title

Advertisement for records of Ida Cox, Alberta Hunter, and Ma Rainey

Description

This advertisement depicts new records out by three "blues queens": Ida Cox, Alberta Hunter, and Ma Rainey. The fact that this ad grouped these three women together suggests that they were viewed as the dominant artists in the "race" or "colored" records market of the mid-1920s.

Date

February 2, 1924

Library of Congress Chronicling America
