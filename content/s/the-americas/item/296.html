Title

Advertisement for "When Old Bill Bailey Plays the Ukulele"

Description

Nora Bayes' hit song "When Old Bill Bailey Plays the Ukulele" is advertized here under "Broadway's All Star Bill." This song, performed on the minstrel stage, plays up the Hawaiian fad of the early 1910s.
