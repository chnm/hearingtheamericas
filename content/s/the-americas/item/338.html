Title

Advertisement for John McCormack's recordings with Victor Records

Description

Victor Records advertizes Italian opera star John McCormack's new records. Three of the records were "operatic airs" and three were Irish ballads, exemplifying the range of genres McCormack recorded.

Date

March 28, 1910

Source

Library of Congress Chronicling America
