Description

Sam Moore, the performer of this remarkable song, was born in Florida. Moore played an eight string version of the Hawaiian steel guitar called the Octo-chorda. His performance sits somewhere between Hawaiian music, “country” music, and the ragtime blues. It makes it easy to see how the Hawaiian steel guitar could lead to both blues and to country music.
