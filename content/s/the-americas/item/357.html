Title

Newspaper article about song "I Ain't Got Nobody"

Description

A newspaper clip announces the new record "I Ain't Got Nobody Much (And Nobody Cares for Me)," which was quite similar to but slightly divergent from the original version of the song "I Ain't Got Nobody Much." Both versions were written by Roger Graham and Spencer Williams.

Date

05-02-1917

Source

University of Illinois Digital Newspaper Collections,
New York Clipper Collection
