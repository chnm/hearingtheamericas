Description

July Paka, a Hawaiian native, and his wife "Toots" Paka, a professional dancer from the US who had never visited Hawaii, led a very successful touring company and may have done more than any other band to popularize Hawaiian music. This song features steel guitar and Ukulele and Hawaiian lyrics
