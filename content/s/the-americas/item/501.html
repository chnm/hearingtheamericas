Title

Finnish newspaper performance advertisement

Description

Here, a Finnish newspaper advertizes Viola Turpeinen's performance at a local hall in Duluth, Minnesota. This speaks to how immigrants in the U.S. desired, produced, and found community around the music of their homelands.

Date

May 3, 1930

Source

Library of Congress American Folklife Center,
