Timeline slide images that were hot-linked to third-party hosts.

The home page's timeline was authored in a Google Sheet and rendered by
TimelineJS loaded from KnightLab's CDN. Its slide images came from two places:
the site's own Omeka files, which are in the object bucket under
/files/original/, and the eight images below, which the sheet pointed at other
people's servers. All eight still resolved when this archive was built. They
are copied here so that the timeline depends on neither the sheet, nor the
CDN, nor those eight hosts.

Six are Wikimedia Commons files and one is a Library of Congress IIIF
derivative; both are stable, openly licensed sources, and the local copies are
byte-for-byte what those hosts returned. The eighth,
11-perry-bradford-publishing-co.jpg, comes from friendsofmusichall.org, a
third party whose terms are not stated -- it was hot-linked by the original
site and is mirrored here on the same footing. Confirming its licence is a
curatorial follow-up.
